The New Turing Test

Response02 / 03

This is a reading response to the article, which discusses AI-generated content and the importance of creating work that feels personal, intentional, and distinctly human.

I deeply resonated with the phrase “From someone, for someone, in a particular context” because it made me reconsider what design can do, especially in this age of AI.

As a designer, I have always felt both threatened and amazed by AI’s creativity and speed, but the article shifted my perspective by reminding me that purposeful design comes from personality, a specific audience, and personal narrative.
