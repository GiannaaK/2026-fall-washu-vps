Why Restaurant Websites…

Response01 / 03
SourceAre.na Editorial ↗

This is a reading response to the article, which discusses restaurant websites and how they were designed and experienced in 2019.

What stood out to me most from the reading was how a restaurant website can shape my impression of a place before I even visit.

I personally experienced this while studying abroad in Florence and traveling with friends. We often compared restaurant websites, menus, social media, and reviews, and I noticed that modern websites felt more polished while older, more traditional ones sometimes felt more local and authentic.

This made me see our project less as creating twenty different styles and more as creating twenty different interpretations of the same restaurant through design.
