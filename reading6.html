Reading Response 6.

There are No Gardens on the Internet

Response06 / 06
SourceHyundai Artlab ↗

This is a experience response to the media, how digital metaphors and interactive experiences shape the way we navigate websites.

Chia Amisola’s website as a garden concept encourages exploration rather than following a fixed path. Clicking through different elements felt more like wandering through a space or playing a game than browsing a typical website. It made me realize that a metaphor can shape not only a website’s visual identity but also how people experience and interact with it. I even found myself rereading descriptions of certain elements simply because they were being added to my inventory, which made the content feel more personal and engaging. As I develop my own website container, I want to create a space that guides visitors while still leaving room for curiosity and unexpected discoveries.
