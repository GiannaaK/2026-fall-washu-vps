Reading Response 3

This is a reading response to the article, which reimagines personal websites as evolving spaces for self-expression.

As a designer who is currently building and constantly refining my portfolio website for post-graduation job applications, this article reminded me of what my portfolio should really be about. It inspired me to focus less on the formats, projects, and styles I think recruiters want to see, and instead use my website as a space to explore and express who I am as a designer and as an individual.
