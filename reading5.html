Reading Response 5.

The Forbidden Zone

Response05 / 05
SourceAre.na Editorial ↗

This is a reading response to the Article, which explores how collecting and organizing can become creative practices that inspire unexpected connections.

Having collected my own shimmering points before this reading, I strongly related to Damon Zucconi’s idea that creating a container can inspire us to find things to fill it, rather than simply organizing what we already have. I’ve always thought of collecting references, like creating a Pinterest board, as a step before designing, but this reading made me realize that collecting and organizing can be creative processes themselves.

I also noticed that my 15 shimmering points shared a certain style and mood that I hadn’t recognized until I brought them together in one space. Discovering these unexpected connections was a surprisingly fun and meaningful part of the process.
