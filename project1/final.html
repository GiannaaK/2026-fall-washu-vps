OUR STORY 우리의 이야기

K-BOP started with one obsession: the Korean cupbop we ate standing on Seoul street corners at 2am after a night out. That chaotic, delicious, saucy cup of everything good.

We brought it to St. Louis, dialed up the spice levels, and added enough options that even your picky friend can find something to love. You're welcome, everyone.
