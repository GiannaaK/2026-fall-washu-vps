What do you want to learn in this course?

I want to learn how to turn my visual design ideas into websites that actually work. As a design student, I am used to thinking about layout, typography, branding, and user experience, but I want to become more comfortable with the technical side of creating for the web. I’m especially interested in learning how coding can become part of my design process rather than something separate from it.

Tell us about an early experience with computers or the internet

One of my earliest memories of using the internet was playing online games and exploring websites as a kid. I was always interested in how different websites looked and felt, even before I really understood what design was. For games, I especially liked customizing profiles, choosing colors and images, and decorating my village and character. Looking back, I think those experiences were some of my first introductions to digital design and probably influenced why I became interested in visual communication.

What will the web look like in 10 years from now?

I think the web will become much more personalized, interactive, and integrated into everyday life. Instead of only visiting websites through a screen, we may interact with digital information through AI assistants, wearable devices, and other environments. At the same time, I hope designers will think more carefully about privacy, transparency, and how this highly-personalized technology affects people emotionally. I think the biggest challenge will be making technology feel more helpful and human without allowing it to become overwhelming or manipulative.
